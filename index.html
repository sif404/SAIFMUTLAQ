A field that senses, thinks, and waters itself.

Across a mid-size open field, capacitive soil-moisture probes and a weather node sit beside every irrigation block. An ESP32 edge controller reads them every few minutes, and the readings travel — wirelessly, automatically — into a pipeline that ends with a real decision: open a valve, hold off, or flag a stressed zone for the agronomist.

This is precision agriculture in miniature: the same architecture running on commercial farms today, condensed into one ten-second run so you can watch every stage happen.

Why combine AI with IoT in the field?

Sensors alone only collect numbers. AI is what turns those numbers into water saved and yield protected.

Real-time awareness
The field's condition is known minute to minute, not discovered on the next walkthrough.
Predictive, not reactive
AI forecasts moisture drop and stress hours ahead, instead of waiting for wilting to appear.
Automated response
Irrigation valves open the moment a threshold is crossed — no one has to be watching.
Less water waste
Zones are watered by actual need, not a fixed timer that ignores rain or soil type.
Scales across hectares
The same pipeline runs unchanged whether it's one sensor cluster or a thousand.
Earlier stress detection
Patterns invisible to the eye — early drought stress, uneven drainage — surface days sooner.
